TenneT is a leading European transmission system operator (TSO) responsible for high‑voltage electricity grids in the Netherlands and Germany. It ensures reliable, secure power supply, drives the energy transition, and provides market platforms for electricity trading. The company focuses on sustainability, grid expansion, and digital innovation to support a low‑carbon future.

**Areas:** Energy · Transmission · Grid Operator · Europe · Sustainability · Company

## How this profile is made

API Evangelist profiles a company's public API surface the way a developer would find it: the website, developer portal and documentation, the specifications it publishes, its public repositories, and its pricing, status and changelog pages. From that it derives the properties above — contracts split per API, workflows, schemas, collections, governance rules, plans and rate limits — and scores them against the published [Kin Score rubric](https://github.com/api-evangelist/kin-score).

The artifacts are published on [APIs.io](https://apis.io), not in this repository. This repository is the public record for TenneT: the place to say something is wrong, missing or out of date, and to ask for a re-score or a removal.
